Cut out the cards below
🐺 COYOTE'S ACME CARDS
Cut out the cards above. Shuffle them. Coyote starts with 2 cards.
🐦 ROAD RUNNER'S ESCAPE CARDS
Cut out the cards above. Shuffle them. Road Runner starts with 2 cards.
HOW TO PLAY
1
Put both tokens on space 1 (GO!). Each player draws 2 cards from their own deck.
2
Youngest player is Road Runner 🐦. Other player is Coyote 🐺.
3
Roll the die. Move your token forward that many spaces along the path.
4
Apply your space's effect:
🌵 Green = jump ahead 3 more
🏜️ Red = go back 3 spaces
📦 Blue = draw 1 card from your deck
5
Play a card (optional). Read it aloud and do what it says. Then it's gone.
6
Pass the die to the other player. Their turn begins!
🏆 HOW TO WIN
🐦 Road Runner wins by reaching the gold FINISH space (space 48)!

🐺 Coyote wins by landing on Road Runner's exact space 2 times! Each time counts as a catch. Road Runner slides back 3 spaces per catch.
